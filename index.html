Don't Panic · a quiet moment

That sinking, something's-coming feeling.

Whatever triggered it — the night before work, a hard morning, a knot in your chest at 3pm — it's a well-documented pattern, not a defect in you. It crests, then it fades. This isn't a problem to solve; it's a moment to move through. Two minutes, one small step at a time.
