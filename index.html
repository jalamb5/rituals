Welcome to Rituals

Rituals conducts your Rise, task-close and Shutdown. Tasks live in Super Productivity; the record lives in your Obsidian daily note. Rituals stores nothing else.

No REST API yet? Install the plugin and copy its key. Rituals degrades to copy/download without it.

Shutdown · 1/2

What's still open?

The loops running in the back of your mind. Write them as thoughts — not tasks. You are not planning tomorrow; you are releasing today. Anything you truly must not forget is carried to tomorrow's Rise.

Shutdown · 2/2

What went right?

One or two real things — not achievements, moments. Skim it if today was rough; this is a light close, not an obligation.
